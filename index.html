Privacy Policy

ED ACHIEVERS – CTET PAPER PYQs 2021–2025

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ED ACHIEVERS ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the ED ACHIEVERS CTET PAPER PYQs mobile application ("App").

The App provides CTET previous-year question practice, educational resources, quizzes, mock tests, learning progress, and optional premium subscription features.

Please read this policy carefully. The information actually collected depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the App. This policy must be updated whenever our data practices change.

1. Information We May Collect

Account Information

If you sign in using Google or email and password, Firebase Authentication may process information such as your email address, authentication identifier, display name, and profile image, where available. We use this information to authenticate users and maintain account access.

Guest Accounts

If guest access is enabled, the App may create an anonymous account identifier to maintain a temporary user session and associated learning progress.

Learning and App Activity

Depending on enabled features, the App may store question attempts, quiz results, mock-test scores, bookmarks, selected subjects, learning progress, and app preferences. This information supports learning features and progress tracking.

Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription, Google Play processes the payment. The App or its backend may receive and process purchase identifiers, product IDs, subscription status, entitlement status, and subscription expiry information to verify and provide premium access.

We do not collect or store your full payment card number, card security code, or Google account password through our App. Payment processing is handled by Google Play under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Technical Information

Firebase and other enabled service providers may process technical information such as app diagnostics, crash reports, device or app identifiers, IP address, and service logs as applicable to the services used. The precise information depends on the configuration and features enabled.

AI Features

If AI assistance is enabled, the question, text, or prompt you submit may be sent to the configured AI service to generate explanations, study guidance, or practice questions. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, or other sensitive personal information in AI prompts.

Information may be used to:

  • Provide account registration, sign-in, and account management.
  • Deliver CTET questions, quizzes, mock tests, and explanations.
  • Save bookmarks, learning history, and progress.
  • Verify purchases and manage premium subscription access.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose errors, improve performance, and maintain app reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and reports of incorrect content.
  • Provide AI-assisted educational features when enabled.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
